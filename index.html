This tool works ONLY on JanitorAI lorebooks NOT advanced scripts. Upload your JanitorAI lorebook information to the 'Source' and press 'Convert'. The output should be ready to import into ST. Anything the source doesn't give a rule for is preserved under extensions.janitorai_source. Everything below "Advanced settings" is adjustable. Remember to always double check the output and make your own adjustments.
